# A Cypherpunk's Manifesto

- **Author:** Eric Hughes
- **Date:** 9 March 1993
- **Tags:** foundational, privacy, philosophy
- **Source:** https://www.activism.net/cypherpunk/manifesto.html
- **License:** Freely distributable historical text, attribution to Eric Hughes. Not formally CC-licensed; treated as free-to-share with attribution.

## Summary
The founding statement of the Cypherpunk movement. Hughes argues that privacy — the power to selectively reveal oneself — is necessary for an open society in the electronic age, and that it cannot be granted by governments or corporations but must be built by individuals using cryptography. Origin of the line **"Cypherpunks write code."**

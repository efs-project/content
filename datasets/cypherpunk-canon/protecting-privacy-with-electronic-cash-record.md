# Protecting Privacy with Electronic Cash

- **Year:** 1993

- **Rights status:** NOASSERTION

## Summary

Finney explains how public-key cryptography, anonymous communication, and
digital cash can preserve the privacy properties of physical cash instead of
turning every transaction into a surveillance record.

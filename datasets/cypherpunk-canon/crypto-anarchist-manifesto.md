# The Crypto Anarchist Manifesto

- **Author:** Timothy C. May
- **Date:** Written mid-1988; distributed at Crypto '88 and the Hackers Conference; read at the September 1992 founding Cypherpunks meeting; posted to the list 22 November 1992.
- **Tags:** foundational, markets, philosophy
- **Source:** https://www.activism.net/cypherpunk/crypto-anarchy.html
- **License:** Freely distributable historical text, attribution to Timothy C. May. Written explicitly to be spread; not formally CC-licensed.

## Summary
May's prophetic 1988 vision that public-key cryptography would let individuals interact anonymously — untraceable communication, reputations over identities, and markets beyond state control. Anticipates digital money and crypto's social consequences a decade early. Closes: *"Arise, you have nothing to lose but your barbed wire fences!"*

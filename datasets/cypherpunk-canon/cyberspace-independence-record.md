# A Declaration of the Independence of Cyberspace

- **Author:** John Perry Barlow
- **Year:** 1996
- **Source:** https://www.eff.org/cyberspace-independence
- **Rights status:** NOASSERTION
- **EFS status:** Link-only; EFF's general license does not establish that this
  Barlow work is covered.

## Summary

Written in Davos after passage of the US Telecommunications Reform Act, the
declaration rejects territorial government sovereignty over online communities
and imagines cyberspace as a self-governing civilization of the mind.

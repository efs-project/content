# Cypherpunk Reading Shelf

This shelf follows the 21-work collection assembled by
[The Cypherpunk Library](https://www.cypherpunkbooks.com/collection), plus
Wei Dai's *b-money*, the proposal cited as reference [1] in the
[Bitcoin whitepaper](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf).

Twelve works are stored in EFS, either as full texts or as PDFs. The other ten
are short records with a summary and a link to an upstream copy.

# A Cypherpunk's Manifesto

- **Author:** Eric Hughes
- **Year:** 1993
- **Source:** https://www.activism.net/cypherpunk/manifesto.html
- **Rights status:** NOASSERTION
- **EFS status:** Link-only; the underlying manifesto is not mirrored without
  an explicit redistribution grant.

## Summary

The founding statement of the cypherpunk movement argues that privacy is the
power to reveal oneself selectively and that private systems must be built with
cryptography and code rather than requested from institutions.

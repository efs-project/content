## About

Near the end of its planetary mission, Voyager 1 turned its cameras back and
took 60 frames for the first "portrait" of the solar system. In this
narrow-angle frame Earth is a crescent only 0.12 pixel across, sitting by
chance inside a ray of sunlight scattered in the camera. The image was taken
through violet, blue and green filters and recombined in colour; the
background features are artifacts of the magnification.

It gave Carl Sagan the title of his 1994 book *Pale Blue Dot: A Vision of the
Human Future in Space*.

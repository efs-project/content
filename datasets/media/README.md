# Media

A small set of images stored on EFS, chosen around one idea: records made to
outlast the medium that carries them.

- **Voyager Golden Record cover** (1977). The engraved cover of the record
  that Voyager 1 and 2 carry into interstellar space, with instructions for
  playing it drawn for a reader who shares no language with us.
- **Pale Blue Dot** (1990). Earth as Voyager 1 saw it from more than 4 billion
  miles away: a crescent smaller than one pixel.
- **Ethereum diamond icon**. The logo of the network these files are stored on,
  as a tiny plain-text SVG.

Each image has a short card beside it with its date, creator, source and
licence. All three are public domain, as recorded on Wikimedia Commons:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Sounds_of_Earth_Record_Cover_-_GPN-2000-001978.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pale_Blue_Dot.png
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ethereum-icon-purple.svg

The Ethereum logo may be a trademark even though the file itself is free of
copyright.

## About

Each Voyager probe carries a 12-inch gold-plated copper record of greetings,
music, natural and human-made sounds, and encoded images from Earth. This gold
aluminium cover protects the record from micrometeorites and also tells a
finder how to play it.

The engraved diagrams show the stylus and playing speed, how to turn the
signal into pictures, a map of 14 pulsars that locates the Sun, and, at the
bottom right, the hydrogen atom's two lowest states, whose transition sets the
unit of time used in the other diagrams. The diagrams are engraved on both
the inner and outer surfaces of the cover, because the outer ones will erode
over time.

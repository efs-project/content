# Ethereum History

Ethereum's story told through its own documents: the posts that announced the project and its launch,
Gavin Wood's essay on Web 3.0, the record of the first Devcon, the emergency notice about The DAO, the
specifications of the DAO fork and The Merge, and two blocks read straight from the chain.

**Start with the [timeline](https://app.efs.eth.limo/explorer/history/timeline.md).** It lists each event
with its date and links to the documents here.

## How to read it

- **Converted posts** keep the author's words unchanged. Each opens with a short header giving the
  original URL and date. Links are kept as published, so some in
  older posts no longer work.
- **EIPs** are the full text from the [ethereum/EIPs](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs) repository,
  public domain under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), with relative links
  made absolute.
- **Block cards** are written for EFS. The values in their tables were read from Ethereum mainnet
  with a JSON-RPC call, and each card names the call so you can repeat the check.

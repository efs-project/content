## Why this is flagged (do not host the PDF without James's sign-off)

In January 2018, Protocol Labs founder Juan Benet publicly alleged that at least nine pages of Tron's
English-language whitepaper were copied — in places nearly verbatim — from the **IPFS** and **Filecoin**
whitepapers, without citation. The IPFS/Filecoin papers were published under a Creative Commons license that
requires attribution, so copying them uncited would also be a license violation. After the accusations,
Tron removed the whitepaper from its official website; Justin Sun attributed the overlap to
Chinese-to-English translation issues.

Because (a) the document's originality and copyright status are disputed and (b) it was withdrawn from the
official source, we do **not** include the PDF. This entry exists to document the coin honestly and to flag
the controversy. If a Tron paper is ever wanted in the dataset, James should source it deliberately and
decide how to caveat it.

## Summary (of the project, not an endorsement of the paper)

Tron is a smart-contract / dApp platform marketed around high throughput and content/entertainment use
cases, using a delegated-proof-of-stake consensus. Its whitepaper is historically significant mainly for
the plagiarism controversy described above rather than for a novel technical contribution.

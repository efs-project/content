## Summary

Tezos is a smart-contract platform whose defining feature is **on-chain governance and self-amendment**:
the protocol can upgrade its own rules through a formal, stake-weighted voting process, avoiding contentious
hard forks. It uses a liquid proof-of-stake consensus (baking) and emphasizes formal verification — its
smart-contract language, Michelson, is designed so contracts can be mathematically reasoned about, which
suits high-value financial use. The position paper frames the philosophy and governance model; the
whitepaper details the ledger and amendment mechanics. Two documents are included because Tezos is
canonically distributed as a whitepaper plus a position paper.

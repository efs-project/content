## Summary

Bitcoin introduced the first practical decentralized digital currency, allowing online payments to be
sent directly between parties without going through a financial institution. Its core contribution is a
solution to the double-spending problem using a peer-to-peer network that timestamps transactions into an
ongoing chain of hash-based proof-of-work, forming a record that cannot be changed without redoing the
work. The longest chain serves both as proof of the sequence of events and as proof that it came from the
largest pool of CPU power, so honest nodes controlling a majority of compute outpace attackers. New coins
are introduced as an incentive for nodes to support the network (mining). The paper laid the foundation
for essentially the entire cryptocurrency field.

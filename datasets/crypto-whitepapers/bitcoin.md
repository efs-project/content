# Bitcoin

- **Project:** Bitcoin
- **Paper:** *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System*
- **Author:** Satoshi Nakamoto (pseudonym)
- **Year:** 2008
- **Canonical source:** https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf
- **File in this folder:** `bitcoin.pdf` (9 pages; byte-identical to the bitcoin.org copy)

## Summary

The Bitcoin whitepaper describes the first practical decentralized digital currency: online payments sent
directly between parties without a financial institution in the middle. Its core contribution is a solution
to the double-spending problem using a peer-to-peer network that timestamps transactions into an ongoing
chain of hash-based proof-of-work. The longest chain serves both as the record of what happened and as proof
that it came from the largest pool of CPU power, so as long as honest nodes control most of that power they
outpace attackers.

New coins are issued as a reward to the nodes that do this work (mining), which gives them an incentive to
keep the network honest. The network launched in January 2009, and the nine-page paper became the starting
point for the whole cryptocurrency field.

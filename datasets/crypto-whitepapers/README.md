# Crypto Whitepapers

The founding papers of major blockchain projects, from Bitcoin's nine pages in 2008 to the Ethereum Yellow
Paper and the Uniswap papers. Each paper sits beside a short **About** card that says who wrote it, when,
where the copy comes from, and what it proposes. Start with a card if you want the two-minute version.

Files are tagged rather than sorted into folders, so one paper can sit in several categories at once
(for example [`ethereum`](https://app.efs.eth.limo/explorer/whitepapers?tags=ethereum),
[`proof-of-stake`](https://app.efs.eth.limo/explorer/whitepapers?tags=proof-of-stake),
[`privacy`](https://app.efs.eth.limo/explorer/whitepapers?tags=privacy) or
[`defi`](https://app.efs.eth.limo/explorer/whitepapers?tags=defi)).

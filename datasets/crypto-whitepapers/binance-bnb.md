## Summary

BNB was launched in July 2017 by the Binance cryptocurrency exchange, founded that year by Changpeng Zhao
and Yi He. It began as a token on Ethereum used to pay fees on the exchange, and Binance raised about
$15 million selling it in an initial coin offering. In 2019 BNB moved to Binance's own Binance Chain, and in
September 2020 Binance launched Binance Smart Chain, an Ethereum-compatible chain built from go-ethereum.
Both chains were renamed under the BNB Chain brand in 2022.

The whitepaper describes BNB Smart Chain's **Proof of Staked Authority** consensus, which combines
delegated proof-of-stake with proof-of-authority: BNB holders stake to elect a small set of validators,
which take turns producing blocks. It also covers EVM compatibility, staking and governance, and a mechanism
that burns part of each block's gas fees. The original Binance Chain (later BNB Beacon Chain) was shut down
in December 2024 after its functions were merged into BNB Smart Chain.

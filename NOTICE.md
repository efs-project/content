# Notice

This repository contains third-party works curated for Ethereum File System.

EFS does not claim ownership of third-party content and does not relicense it. Reuse, redistribution, pinning, or mirroring must follow each item's recorded license or distribution terms.

For each item, preserve:

- title
- author or rights holder
- source URL
- license or distribution terms
- copyright notice, when provided
- modification status: verbatim, converted, summarized, or link-only
- EFS hosting status: allowed, link-only, review-required, or not-allowed

The current datasets were assembled quickly for a visible EFS demo. Some machine-readable rights metadata still lives in dataset READMEs or per-file markdown pages rather than in every manifest entry. Treat those notes as authoritative until the manifests are fully normalized.

## Current Rights Posture

| Dataset | Status |
|---|---|
| `web-games` | Mostly CC0-1.0 and MIT single-file games. Preserve MIT notices in the files. |
| `ethereum-eips` | CC0-1.0. EIPs/ERCs are public-domain per the EIP process. |
| `cypherpunk-canon` | Mixed. Three works are included under recorded public-domain or CC BY-SA terms. Eighteen additional works are represented only by CC0 metadata records because their bytes are not cleared for EFS mirroring. |
| `crypto-whitepapers` | Mixed. Some files are freely redistributable, some are academic/public references, and some entries are link-only because rights are unclear. Review per-file notes before broad redistribution. |
